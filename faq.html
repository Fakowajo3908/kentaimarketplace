Kentai Marketplace Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to our FAQ section! Here you'll find answers to common questions about Kentai Marketplace. If you can't find what you're looking for, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.

General Questions

Q: What is Kentai Marketplace?

A: Kentai Marketplace is an online marketplace designed for buying, selling, and promoting a wide variety of products. We connect users who want to find great deals and give new life to pre-loved items.

Q: How do I create an account?

A: To create an account, click on the "Sign Up" or "Register" button, usually located in the top right corner of the homepage. You'll need to provide your email address and create a password.

Q: Is Kentai Marketplace free to use?

A: Creating an account and browsing listings on Kentai Marketplace is free. We may have fees associated with selling items or promoting listings, which will be clearly outlined in our Terms and Conditions.

Buying on Kentai Marketplace

Q: How do I buy an item?

A: To buy an item, simply browse our listings, click on the item you're interested in, and follow the instructions to make a purchase. You'll typically communicate with the seller to arrange payment and delivery.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?

A: Payment methods are usually arranged directly between the buyer and seller. Kentai Marketplace facilitates the connection but does not directly handle payments. We recommend using secure payment methods.

Q: What if I have an issue with a purchase?

A: If you encounter an issue with a purchase, we encourage you to first communicate directly with the seller to resolve the problem. If a resolution cannot be reached, please contact our support team for assistance.

Selling on Kentai Marketplace

Q: How do I sell an item?

A: To sell an item, you need to have an account. Once logged in, click on the "Sell" or "Post an Item" button. You'll be guided through the process of creating a listing, including adding photos, descriptions, and pricing.

Q: Are there fees for selling?

A: There may be fees associated with listing or selling items on KentaiMIA. These fees, if any, will be clearly communicated to you during the listing process and in our Terms and Conditions.

Q: How do I promote my listings?

A: Kentai Marketplace offers options to promote your listings to increase their visibility. You can find more information on promotion options on your seller dashboard or the "Promote" page.

Q: How does Kentai Marketplace protect my privacy?

A: We take your privacy seriously. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for detailed information on how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.
